Menswear Collection

Equipped with specialized automated machinery for crisp collars and precise seaming, our menswear line offers unparalleled durability and sharp aesthetics.

We work with fine cottons, sturdy denims, and blended fabrics to deliver bulk orders that meet exacting retail standards.

Womenswear Collection

Our expansive factory lines cater to complex designs, intricate detailing, and delicate fabrics standard in contemporary womenswear.

From fast-fashion volume runs to premium boutique capsules, we ensure consistency in fit, draping, and finish across every unit.

Combining the latest in textile engineering with durable construction techniques like flatlock stitching and seamless knitting.
